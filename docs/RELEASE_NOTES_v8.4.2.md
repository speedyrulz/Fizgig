**Check a trigger word, before you caption.** At the bottom of the Training tab's Subjects card, a new section (collapsed until you open it) checks a trigger word with the text encoder of the model you're training. Fizgig LoRA Isolation joins each subject's trigger to what the subject is (`zwxem, a woman` trains as `zwxemwoman`), and some words join badly: the encoder splits them so "woman" is no longer a whole word (`fyxo` + woman reads as `f | yx | ow | oman`). The check shows how your word reads, whether it shares a first letter or a piece with your other subjects' triggers, and suggests made-up words that join cleanly. The same word can join well with one subject and break with another (`dlor` + woman is fine, `dlor` + man is not), so it checks with the subject you pick. Thanks to the Reddit commenter who suggested it.
